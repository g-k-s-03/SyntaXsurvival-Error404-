— The process

Blood found in
under 3 minutes

01

Hospital submits request

Selects blood group, urgency level (Critical / Urgent / Normal), units and location. GPS auto-detects or entered manually.

02

Matching engine fires

Filters by blood compatibility, removes ineligible donors, calculates real travel distances, ranks by nearest first.

03

Top 3 donors alerted

Simultaneous SMS sent. Donor taps Accept or Decline. No response in 3 min — next batch auto-alerted.

04

Donor confirmed & arrives

Hospital gets instant SMS confirmation. Donor navigates via Google Maps link. Case marked fulfilled. Donor locked 56 days.
